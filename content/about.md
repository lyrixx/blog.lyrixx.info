I'm Grégoire Pineau, a web tinkerer with ops tendencies, based near Paris. On the internet, I'm [lyrixx](https://github.com/lyrixx).

I have been working at [JoliCode](https://jolicode.com) since 2017, as a developer and ops. I also work at [redirection.io](https://redirection.io) as a tech lead and architect.

## Open source

I'm a member of the Symfony Core Team. I became a core contributor in 2016, after the Workflow component got merged, and I still maintain it. I also contribute a lot to [symfony/ai](https://github.com/symfony/ai).

And I maintain a few other projects:

- [Castor](https://castor.jolicode.com/), a task runner in PHP
- [docker-starter](https://github.com/jolicode/docker-starter), a skeleton to start a web project with PHP, Docker and Castor
- [beberlei/metrics](https://github.com/beberlei/metrics)
- [gitlib](https://github.com/gitonomy/gitlib)
- [consul-php-sdk](https://github.com/FriendsOfPHP/consul-php-sdk)

If you want (and can), you can [sponsor me](https://github.com/sponsors/lyrixx) to continue to work on open source.

## What do I use?

PHP with Symfony, Go, Rust, and JavaScript or TypeScript with React. I like working with PostgreSQL, RabbitMQ, Redis, ClickHouse and Elasticsearch. And for ops, I use Docker, Ansible and Terraform.
